# FunctionFoundry.Scheduling

## Scheduling primitives

### Interval-set algebra

Normalized union, intersection, difference, and complement operations over open/closed UTC intervals.

### Recurring availability

Multi-participant resolution across time zones with DST ambiguity policies, exceptions, minimum durations, and ranked candidates.

### Business calendar

Working-day arithmetic with split hours and caller-supplied closures. Results include explainable step paths.

### Critical-path scheduler

Dependency validation, cycle paths, ES/LS/slack metrics, and optional resource leveling heuristics marked non-optimal.
